Simple Weather Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Publisher: Simple Weather
Contact: rara@dinowing.com

This is a test version for TestFlight. We will update it before public release.

The short version

  • We have no accounts. We do not ask for your name or email.
  • We use your location to show your forecast. We do not sell it.
  • We collect basic usage counts to learn which screens people use.
  • Most of what the app remembers stays on your phone.

What the app uses

Location. With your permission, the app reads your phone's location while you use it. It sends your coordinates to weather services to get your forecast (see "Who receives data"). You can choose a town by hand instead. You can turn location off in iPhone Settings at any time.

Home place. The app saves a home place on your phone. It never leaves the phone.

Badges, records and settings. The app saves your badges, daily readings, activity picks and settings on your phone. We cannot see them.

Usage data. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count events such as opening the app, answering the location question and switching to Kid view. Events use fixed words. They never contain place names or text you type. Google receives a device identifier, these event counts and your approximate location (city level). We do not use the advertising identifier. We do not track you across other companies' apps or sites. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions, the app asks for your consent before it sends any usage data. You can turn it off in the app's menu.

Calendar. The app does not read your calendar.

Who receives data

  • Open-Meteo receives the coordinates of the place you look up, to return the forecast and air quality.
  • The U.S. National Weather Service receives the coordinates of the place you look up, to return official alerts.
  • Zippopotam receives a ZIP code when you search by ZIP code.
  • Apple handles place name search (Apple Maps) and location services on your phone.
  • Google (Firebase) receives usage data as described above.

These services see your IP address, as any internet service does. We do not control their policies. We do not receive a copy of your location.

Children

Kid view is for a child to look at with a parent. It sends only the same forecast request as the rest of the app. It collects nothing about the child. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn location off in iPhone Settings, then pick a town by hand.
  • Turn off "Share usage data" in the app's menu.
  • Delete the app to remove everything saved on your phone.
  • To ask about usage data tied to your device, email us at the contact above.

Keeping data

Usage data stays in Google Analytics for 2 months. Everything else stays on your phone.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date.
